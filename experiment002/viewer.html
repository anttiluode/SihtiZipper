SIHTIZIPPER · EXPERIMENT 002 · TRAINED MINI-DDPM

Pixels imagined. Relationships preserved.

These are two actual 16-frame test sequences from a 24-video frozen evaluation. A vertical screen hides two colored objects; one test video reverses their motion during occlusion. The diffusion neural net was trained on separate videos. The zipper-style tracker keeps explicit temporal identities and may reject false observations. This is a controlled toy experiment, not natural video or a general comparison with state-of-the-art diffusion.

01 / Hidden truth (test only)

The unoccluded clean video. Used for scoring, not shown to inference.

02 / Camera observation

Opaque vertical screen, sensor noise and occasional false highlights. Shared information.

03 / Diffusion, raw

18 steps of a genuinely trained pixel DDPM. Better foreground reproduction, but background hallucinations.

04 / Diffusion, calibrated

Same DDPM, with an offset selected on 24 independent validation videos. Often erases the object too.

05 / Constant velocity, no gate

Long-lived identity tracks, but forced acceptance of misleading camera observations.

06 / Zipper-style jam and relock

Separate object tracks, a jam/abstain state, and no rewriting of older observations.

Do not compare unlike claims. Pixels are scored by unseen-region MSE. Accepted associations and false joins are scored only for the trackers. The diffusion model makes pictures, not explicit observed-identity assertions. The tracker has a longer remembered state than the DDPM's two-frame conditioning window.
